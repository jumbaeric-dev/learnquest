# 1. Project Overview

LearnQuest is a modern, future-focused digital learning platform designed to prepare children for the rapidly changing world.

Unlike traditional e-learning systems that primarily measure course completion, LearnQuest focuses on developing real-world competencies including AI literacy, coding, creativity, communication, critical thinking, digital citizenship, collaboration, and lifelong learning habits.

The platform combines structured learning, gamification, analytics, adaptive learning, and artificial intelligence to provide an engaging learning experience while giving parents and educators meaningful insight into each child's growth.

LearnQuest is being designed as a scalable platform capable of supporting individual learners, schools, teachers, parents, and educational organizations.

To become one of the world's leading platforms for preparing children for the future through engaging, measurable, and personalized learning experiences.

Future readiness is the primary objective of LearnQuest.

Every feature should contribute toward helping children succeed in a technology-driven world.

# 4. Core Principles

Every feature added to LearnQuest should support at least one of these principles.

## Future First

Education should prepare learners for tomorrow, not only today's examinations.

## Learning by Doing

Children learn best through interactive activities rather than passive reading.

## Progress Over Perfection

Celebrate consistent improvement rather than perfect scores.

## Personalization

Every learner progresses differently.

The platform should adapt to each learner whenever possible.

## Transparency

Parents and educators should understand how scores, progress, badges, and recommendations are calculated.

## Scalability

Architectural decisions should support future expansion without major redesign.

# 9. Architectural Philosophy

LearnQuest follows a service-oriented architecture.

Business logic belongs inside dedicated service classes.

Models represent data.

Resources manage administration.

Widgets provide analytics.

Controllers and Livewire components remain thin.

Every major feature should be modular and reusable.

---

# 10. Coding Philosophy

The project emphasizes:

* Readability
* Maintainability
* Consistency
* Reusability
* Testability
* Scalability

The preferred approach is to solve problems through reusable services rather than duplicated logic.

Configuration values should live inside configuration files whenever practical.
